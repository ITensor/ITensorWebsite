ITensor School 2026

Two days of lectures and hands-on coding with ITensors.jl and ITensorMPS.jl
September 21–22, 2026 · Flatiron Institute, New York

When

Monday, September 21 & Tuesday, September 22, 2026
9:00 am – 5:30 pm each day

Where

Flatiron Institute, 2nd Floor Auditorium
162 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY

Before You Arrive

Install Julia on your laptop, then browse the ITensorMPS tutorial pages.

The ITensor School is a hands-on introduction to tensor network methods and the ITensor software, taught by the ITensor developer team. Lectures cover matrix product states and DMRG, time evolution, the internals of ITensors.jl, quantics tensor trains and learning with MPS, and tensor networks beyond one dimension using belief propagation. Each lecture block is followed by a hands-on session where you run and modify real ITensor code.

Lectures are 45 minutes plus 15 minutes for questions. Slides will be linked from each lecture as the school proceeds.
